On 2026-10-19 the user rejected all three reels made in one batch (decoy effect, Flipkart ad breakdown, AI chip selloff). Their words: "even I am the subject matter expert, I didn't understand", "no head, no foot", "so basic, so non-understandable, non-connecting", and "you used typography and basic typography animation that I banned".

**Why:** A reel lives or dies in the first 6-7 seconds, and the audience is laymen. Batch production traded quality for quantity.

**How to apply:**
- **One reel at a time.** Write the script in plain words first and share it with the user (hook, story, visuals) before building. Don't batch.
- **The hook (0-6 s) must make the subject unmistakable with real visuals:** the real brand (logo, product, ad clip or real footage), not abstract shapes, a blank screen or stick figures. A viewer must know "this is about Flipkart / AI stocks / pricing" instantly.
- **English only.** No Hindi lines on screen or in the voiceover (translate, e.g. "buy one size bigger").
- **Layman storytelling:** one clear idea, a relatable everyday example, cause and effect spelled out. Test every line with "would my non-tech friend get this?". No jargon, no chains of numbers, no academic citations as the payoff.
- **Visual quality:** real footage, real product UIs, real logos, cinematic motion and 3D. Typography-led scenes, word bubbles, simple cards and bar charts as the main visual all count as banned "basic typography".
- Use every available tool (Remotion skills, stock footage via [[stock-footage-tool]], MoneyPrinterTurbo, other repos) to raise quality.
