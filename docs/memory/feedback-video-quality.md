Videos must be energetic and premium ("Marvel / institutional level", "beat Manus"), never slow or mid-level; music with adrenaline, cuts on the beat; colourful but strictly inside the brand palette; **no overlapping elements**; busy moments (several cards or names animating) slow enough to read, and they accept a few extra seconds for that; on-screen copy exactly as scripted, no em or en dashes.

**Why:** v1 of the Abstraxn film was "too slow, like slow piano" and "mid-level"; later versions had 2 to 3 overlaps and rushed card animations, each called out.

**How to apply:** run the repo's `.claude/skills/rewires-video-qa` checklist on styleframes before the full render and on a contact sheet of the final file before delivering. Related: [[feedback-delivery-workflow]].
