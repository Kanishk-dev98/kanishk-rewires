On 2026-10-22 the user was disappointed with the sales-machine reel:
1. The on-screen first slide should carry a designed headline of the hook (e.g. "Build your advanced sales team with Claude Code") while the voice says the spoken hook. Don't open on an unrelated prop.
2. The whole video was built on a single animated funnel that looked immature and toy-like. Show a funnel (or any metaphor object) only in the beats that need it, and make it mature and realistic, like real premium funnel visuals (research references online).
3. Several places had number and word treatments that looked like "typical AI": floating chips, "EXAMPLE" tags everywhere, counters in odd boxes. They want high-grade, Marvel-level production.
4. Push next-level motion and animation while staying inside the brand typography and light palette.

**How to apply:**
- Each beat gets the visual that best fits it. Vary the visual language across beats; never one object for the whole film.
- Metaphor objects must be realistic and refined: correct proportions, materials, lighting and restraint. Study real references first.
- Typography and numbers: one disciplined type scale; numbers set large and clean, with tabular figures and plain labels. Avoid clusters of pills or chips, stray mono tags, and repeated "EXAMPLE" stickers (put one subtle disclaimer in the caption or footer instead).
- Open with a designed hook headline on screen (brand type, one accent), synced to the spoken hook.
