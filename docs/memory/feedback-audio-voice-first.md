On 2026-10-22 the user, reviewing the sales-machine reel, said every video uses "the same type of music" and the same "bomb-type" hits, which is "a sign of typical AI". Music should always be dimmed, and "the main primary authority should be the voice, so it should be audible properly."

**Why:** Repeated stock-sounding music and boom hits make the channel sound templated and AI-made. An unclear voice loses the viewer.

**How to apply:**
- Keep music about 18-22 dB under the voice, with ducking whenever the VO speaks. The voice must be clearly intelligible on phone speakers.
- Give each reel its own music character (tempo, key, instrumentation, texture). Don't reuse the same generated bed across videos. Record the choice in the storyboard.
- Avoid `boom`/`impact` hits by default. Use sparse, subtle sound design (soft ticks, airy whooshes, UI clicks) at low volume, and never on top of spoken words. At most 1-2 accent hits per reel, only at a real turn.
