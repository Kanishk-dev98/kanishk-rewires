On 2026-10-19 the user reviewed the Flipkart ad-breakdown v3. The whole video ran on footage: ad clips plus café, gym and phone stock that felt repetitive, and landscape clips cropped to 9:16 didn't fit well. Their direction: use the footage only where the video talks about the ad (the Flipkart part). Everywhere else, go with our motion-graphics theme or new kinds of motion.

**Why:** Footage-only reels look like generic stock edits and lose the Kanishk.Rewires motion-design identity. Badly cropped 16:9 clips look cheap.

**How to apply:**
- Real footage appears only when it is the literal subject: the ad being analysed, the product being demoed, the event being reported.
- Explanation, lessons, examples and CTAs are custom motion graphics in the brand theme (charcoal or cream, lime accent, real UIs, 3D, kinetic diagrams). Don't fill them with stock B-roll.
- Present 16:9 footage framed for 9:16 (a cinematic card, a device frame or a split layout with graphics) rather than an aggressive center crop.
- Don't repeat the same footage source across scenes.
