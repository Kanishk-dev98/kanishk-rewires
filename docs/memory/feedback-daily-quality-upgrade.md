On 2026-10-19 the user said they were "disappointed very much": the latest batch (Flipkart, Grok Bot, decoy) was lower in quality than the reels posted the day before. Their direction: improve day by day, beat the previous best, upgrade the level of animation, motion graphics and video quality, use very little stock footage, go HD or 4K, and raise the craft of typography. "Otherwise we are going to lose everything."

**Why:** The channel's growth depends on visibly rising production value. One weaker batch damages the brand and the posting rhythm.

**How to apply:**
- Before building, name the best reel so far (the current benchmark) and state what this reel does better: a new motion technique, richer 3D, better transitions, sharper type.
- Never trade quality for quantity or speed. One great reel beats three average ones.
- Motion-led: real 3D, camera moves, layered parallax, particle and light effects, purposeful transitions. Real UIs and brand assets. Stock footage only where it is the subject.
- Typography is crafted, not default: kinetic and masked reveals with spacing and weight contrast. Never typography-only scenes.
- Keep a living reference library: study the user's reference reels and trending top creators, and extract techniques into the rewires-motion-library skill.
- After each reel, run a self-review against the benchmark and record one lesson.
