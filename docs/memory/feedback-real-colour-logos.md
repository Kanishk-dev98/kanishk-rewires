On 2026-10-20 the user was upset (again) that the Harness² reel showed a black, single-colour Google "G". They had told me "multiple times" to use real, colourful logos.

**Why:** Monochrome or recoloured logos look fake and cheap, and viewers recognise brands by their real colours. Simple Icons only provides single-colour glyphs, which is how this slipped through repeatedly.

**How to apply:**
- Never use Simple Icons, or any recoloured or single-tone version, for a brand logo that has an official multi-colour mark. Examples: Google G (blue, red, yellow, green), Gmail M, Google Calendar, Chrome, Gemini sparkle gradient, Slack, Microsoft, Instagram gradient, Figma (5 colours), Canva, Notion (black on white is its real form).
- Get the official full-colour logo from the brand's press or brand-resources page or the official Wikimedia Commons SVG (e.g. `Google_"G"_logo.svg`). Store it in `studio/public/media/logos/` with PROVENANCE.
- Use it unaltered: original colours, proportions and clear space. Place it on a white or neutral tile if needed for contrast. Don't tint it lime or charcoal.
- Before rendering, check every logo on the board: is it the real full-colour mark? If not, fix it before the full render.
- Simple Icons is OK only for brands whose official logo is genuinely single-colour (e.g. X, Apple, Notion), and still in that brand's real colour.
