# Apolio · Personal AI Agent by Abstraxn · 60 s use-case film

**Files:** `Apolio_by_Abstraxn_60s_v1.mp4` (1:00, 1920×1080, 30 fps, −13.3 LUFS, bt709, faststart), `styleframes/` (12 × 1920×1080 PNG), `styleframe_board.jpg`.
**Code:** `studio/src/apolio/` (composition `Apolio60`), music placeholder `pipeline/make_apolio_score.py`.

## Approach

A blend of the Manus 2.0 film grammar (`research/reference-videos/manus-2-0/analysis.md`) and our beat-locked motion system from the 61 s Abstraxn brand film:

- **From Manus:** light airy canvas, calm scenery behind floating UI, small mixed-size type ("One *tap,*" / "You *decide.*"), a word roll (chat → Claude → ChatGPT → any MCP tool), icon + label chapter tags, phones, a character.
- **Beyond Manus:** every cut on the beat, kinetic words landing as they read, the Abstraxn logo-triangle grid as connective tissue (it ripples, explodes and flashes red on the denial), particle bursts, green light-sweep transitions, a dark hero scene with camera shake, signed receipts and a live ETH chart.
- **Apolio** is a character built from the Abstraxn logo triangle (a facet highlight and a triangle "badge"), with blinking eyes and calm, happy and alert moods.
