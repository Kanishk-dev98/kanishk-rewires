## The house system

**1. One canvas, zero cuts.** Every reel measured 0 hard cuts.
- Fixed chrome on every frame:
  - Logo top-left, with a thin progress hairline under it that fills across the reel.
  - A wide-tracked mono **chapter pill** top-right: `STEP 2 · FIND THE PERSON · 04 / 7`, or `OMNIGET · 01 / 09`.
- Only the centre "stage" changes. Chapters run about 6 to 8 s each.
- Inside a chapter, one hero card persists and keeps gaining rows, counters, badges or highlights every 1 to 2 s.
- Chapter changes: a 0.3 to 0.5 s blur/scale cross-dissolve, a hero object transforming (map → card → pipeline → table → site → chat), or connector lines drawing to the next section.
- This is how 45 to 90 s stays watchable without cuts.

**2. Two looks, chosen by pillar.**
- Tools/AI reels: cream/peach mesh gradient (orange top-right blob, mint bottom-left), white cards with big radii and soft shadows.
- Freelancing reels: dark charcoal with a grid.
- One orange-red accent throughout. Green only for ticks and winners, red only for ✕ and rejected.

**3. Headline pairing.** Section titles in heavy black grotesk with one orange **italic serif** word ("Download *anything.*", "= *lost sales.*", "Meet *Kapso*", "Kharcha? *Zero.*"), plus a tiny tracked eyebrow ("✦ DID YOU KNOW?", "✦ THE RESULT · LIVE"). This is very close to our cream + charcoal + serif accent.

**4. Captions sit low-centre** (about 58 to 75% of the height).
- Bold sans, built word by word; the ghosted next word turns from grey to black.
- One keyword per line in a **filled pill**: accent colour for the payoff phrase, black for tool or concept names.
- Script: Devanagari, with English nouns kept in Latin script, or Latin Hinglish.
- Free-tool variant: two text layers, a top section headline plus karaoke at the bottom.
- News variant: a full-sentence paper strip with a highlighter. It underperformed.

**5. Hook = a number on screen in the first second, proved live.**
- 0 → 20 leads, 1,800+ sites, a countdown ring to 0, 312 businesses, 18,33,463 stars (lakh grouping), a ~~₹30,000~~ → ₹55,000 strike-and-replace.
- The voice starts at 0.0 s.

**6. Concrete, local, named examples, carried through the whole reel.**
- Rohan the cafe owner. Sharma Dental Clinic in Indiranagar. ₹15K to 40K budgets. "Booked Friday 4:30 PM". The "morning chai" review. The friend who always begs for your hotspot.
- Money and time are the units throughout.

**7. Recreated product UI, never screen recordings.**
- ChatGPT, Maps, Sheets, WhatsApp, Upwork, Reddit, X, Gmail, Cal.com, a phone lock screen, terminals.
- Real brand logos. Photoreal object cut-outs (router, phone, laptop) in the free-tool variant, credited in the post caption.

**8. Script spine.**
1. Result-first hook.
2. The old way or the pain (3 to 6 s).
3. A one-line insight or reframe ("cold email vs public post", "Hiring = Budget + Urgency", "send the site, not a pitch").
4. Numbered steps, one chapter each, each with its named tool and logo. A visible table of contents up front for list reels.
5. A funnel or result number.
6. An honest caveat as the twist: "not prediction, rehearsal", "one catch", "help first, not pitch", "rules before you ship".
7. CTA.

Delivery is first person ("maine banaya", I built it), at 115 to 166 wpm, with about 0.4 s breaths placed on chapter turns.

**9. The CTA as an event, with an oversized payload.**
- A comment box types the KEYWORD, then a DM toast arrives with the promised reward.
- The reward is bigger than the reel: "4 prompts + DM template", "top 15 list + install commands", "full setup PDF + prompts".
- Keywords are emotional outcome words or the tool name: CLIENT, LEADS, MAPS, SKILLS, WHATSAPP, MERGE, OMNIGET, PROMPTS.
- Secondary action, chosen from:
  - "Send this to the friend who…" (a named relationship). This drives shares.
  - A Part 2 tease by name.
  - "Day 12 tomorrow".
  - "Road to 10K" follow/subscribe. They spend 3 to 11 s on it, which is dead air for non-followers.

**10. Post caption.**
- Hook line, then the comment-for-DM line (often line 2).
- The beats as a numbered or @-tagged flow.
- "What's in the PDF" as ✅ ticks.
- A trust line, e.g. "I review and send emails myself, not auto-spam".
- "Save karo" (save it) and "send to a friend".
- Source credits.
- 5 to 14 hashtags.

**11. Audio.** A calm male voice (likely cloned or TTS: no breath gaps, tight edits) over a ducked bed.
- The music margin varies from about 1 to 10 dB. Their best-mixed reel (Deq8) kept music about 10 dB under; others sit too close.
- Light UI sound effects: ticks, typing, chimes, stamps, the WhatsApp tone.

## Failure modes seen here

- Pure news with no personal gain, in an off-brand template: 25 comments.
- More than 4 tools per reel, or more than 160 wpm, overloads the viewer.
- 12 items in 90 s is too long. Cap list reels at 5 to 7 items, about 4 s each.
- Double follow-gates (IG and YouTube) and 10 s growth pleas.
- Realistic fake posts or names with no "example" label.
- Piracy-adjacent tools.
- Dense terminal frames that can't be read at speed.
