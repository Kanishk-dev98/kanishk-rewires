## Motion feel

Everything enters with a blur-to-sharp fade and a fast ease-out. Hard cuts alternate between cream and black. There is one accent word per line, and it's an italic serif. The text is small and elegant with lots of negative space, and something tiny is always moving (specks, cursors, spinners).
