## The style grammar

1. **Light and airy.** Off-white canvas (#F5F5F5-ish), black and dark-grey type, one blue accent. Dark frames are rare and used for emphasis (the title, the "New Studio" word roll, the QR punchline).
2. **Small, calm type cards**, centred, medium weight, lots of empty space: "See what's new", "Higher quality", "Put it to work.", "Remote control". Emphasis comes from **mixed sizes and baseline shifts** ("See what's *new*" with "new" smaller and raised in blue; "Manus gives you the *power* to build"), not from bigness.
3. **Word roll / slot machine.** A vertical list of words scrolls past a fixed word ("New / Level, App, **Studio**, Architecture, Manus").
4. **Section headers = icon + label** ("▶ Create videos", "🎮 Build Games", "Automations"). They chapter the film without a narrator.
5. **Real product UI, big and clean.** Prompts typing into the composer, cursor clicks, the timeline editor, dialogs. The camera pushes and pans into regions of the UI; the UI is never a static screenshot.
6. **UI floating over serene nature photography.** Glaciers, salt flats, desert lakes: calm, cool, high-key. A glass card or phone floats on top. This is the signature look.
7. **Output showcases full bleed.** The videos and games Manus made play at full frame between the UI moments, showing results rather than features.
8. **Phones and real life.** Phone mockups, a QR code in a café window, a hand holding a phone at night: the product placed in the world.
9. **Story beats:** what's new → capability chapters (video, games, automations, cloud computer, remote control) → "One More Thing" (an Apple homage) → a playful mascot reveal (Cue) → real-world use case (QR ordering) → thesis ("Manus gives you the power to build / You decide what's worth building") → logo with mascots.
10. **Pacing:** relaxed but never idle, 3 to 4 s per shot, cuts on the beat, little motion blur, soft spring easing, gentle zooms.

## What to borrow for a style study (not a copy)

Take the grammar, not the assets: the light canvas, small mixed-size type, the word roll, icon chapter cards, UI over calm scenery, full-bleed outputs, the "one more thing" turn and a thesis close. Never reuse Manus footage, mascots, logo or wording.
