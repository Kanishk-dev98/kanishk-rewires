# Instagram landscape: findings (2026-10-14)

Source: four parallel research passes, each candidate fetched live on instagram.com. Full list: `accounts.md` / `accounts.json` / `accounts.html` (84 verified accounts). Published page: https://claude.ai/artifact/KrXogtoFXFPymkn3Yx9ZQC

## The gap

- The largest **faceless** AI pages (evolving.ai 4.8M, chatgptricks 2.7M, airesearches 1.4M) and the big **data publishers** (Statista, Visual Capitalist, Our World in Data) mostly post **static images and carousels**, not reels.
- Almost every **reel-first** AI or marketing account is **creator-led** (talking head + graphics).
- Only Bloomberg Business among data publishers posts mostly reels.
- **No account checked runs a faceless, high-volume channel of animated data stories about AI + marketing.** That is exactly what this engine produces.
