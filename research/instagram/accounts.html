84 accounts, and the gap none of them fill

Every handle below was opened on its live Instagram page and confirmed active, with follower counts as shown that day. Use it to study formats, then pick a name and niche. Accounts marked ★ are the closest references for motion-graphics AI and marketing reels.

Carousels

The biggest faceless AI pages (evolving.ai, chatgptricks, airesearches) and data publishers (Statista, Visual Capitalist) mostly post static posts and carousels, not reels.

Faces

Almost every reel-first AI or marketing account is creator-led: someone talking to camera. Faceless motion graphics at volume is rare.

The opening

No account checked runs a faceless, high-volume channel of animated data stories about AI and marketing. That is exactly what your engine produces.

Name & niche starters

Ideas shaped by the gap above. I have not checked whether these handles are free on Instagram; check before you decide.

The Growth Graph@thegrowthgraph

Animated data stories on how brands, apps and AI products grow. Direct home for the "Race to 100M" format.

Hundred Million@hundredmillion.ai

Every reel is a numbers story: who hit scale, how fast, and what it cost. Strong recall from your first video.

Marketing in Motion@marketinginmotion

Marketing case studies and campaign breakdowns as kinetic motion graphics. Fills the reels gap left by Marketing Mind and Because of Marketing.

AI, Explained in Numbers@ai.innumbers

AI news told only through data: funding, users, cost per token, benchmarks. Faceless, cinematic, fact-checked.

Brand Math@brandmath

"Why did this work?" breakdowns of brands and ads with the numbers on screen. India-first angle possible.

The Scale Story@thescalestory

Mix of AI and marketing stories framed as growth races and turning points. Broadest of the six.

Method: four research passes (AI explainers, marketing case studies, data visualization, brands and agencies). Candidates came from published ranking lists (NoGood, Scrumball, Apify, Heepsy, Qoruz) and category knowledge, then each was fetched on instagram.com. Format notes come from bios and the reel-to-carousel mix of recent posts, not from watching each reel, so watch three or four before copying a style. Handles that were blocked, private, or belonged to someone else were left out.
