# Story structures (as craft: pacing, beat timing, what sits in each slot)

Content and claims come from the brief. This file gives the shapes that work, with timing ratios measured from the reference sets.

## 1. Spine rules every strong film follows

1. **The character or the use case first, never the logo alone.** A greeting by 1 s; a typed ask over a real context by 0.4 s; a question answered "Yes" by 4 s; a number by 7 s. A cold viewer can state the problem by 5 s.
2. **Context first.** The first seconds tell a cold viewer what problem this is, what they will see and why it matters; never open straight into a comparison or a feature.
3. **Problem as one physical metaphor, 2 to 6 s**, not a stock montage: a grey diagram unified by a sweeping brand shape (problem → product in one move); one sentence exploding into six hidden questions; a hairline map growing denser with every pain. Weakest films have no problem beat, or three soft metaphors.
4. **Every claim gets a concrete proof within ~1.5 s.**
5. **One case end to end beats a feature list.** One request followed from start to a number on a record. Breadth films (six functions, 64 s of slides) lose the thread.
6. **Proof escalates**: capability → finding → evidence → action with a number → artefact.
7. **Show one refusal** among approvals; a refusal is what makes rules credible. All-green proves nothing; all-refusals is a story gap.
8. **Signpost chapters**: a silent card in a VO breath, a background colour per chapter, a luminance ramp, or a typed question.
9. **Payoff is an artefact**: a number on a real record, a released order, a signed receipt, the app on a phone.
10. **End on a line + URL, with a hold.** CTA ≥ 4 s (films), URL ≥ 48 px, 1 s hold after the music resolves. End on an install line or URL, never on the logo alone.
11. **A tension beat before the product**: the strongest films name a real problem first.

## 2. Beat sheets

### 2.1 Brand / launch film (60 to 90 s, warm VO + music)

| Beat | Ratio of runtime | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Character / greeting | 1 to 3% | the product or protagonist introduces itself, or the typed ask over a real scene |
| Brand-shape reveal into the world | ~1% | brand-shape window or iris opens onto the world |
| Problem / context | 6 to 15% | one physical metaphor, stated plainly |
| Product acts in continuous takes | 55 to 65% | 2 to 3 pillars, each opened by the product name in the VO (the name works as a chapter marker); chapter changes in VO breaths |
| One dark "machinery" block | 6 to 11%, inside the acts | the under-the-hood proof (dive into a chip → chain with one calm denial → surface with the verdict) |
| Payoff / human beat | 15 to 20% | the artefact, the human approving, the result |
| Tag + logo + URL | 6 to 10% | breath-and-hit, word-locked line, lockup, CTA with hold |

Hero (held) moment at **35 to 40% of runtime** when possible; consider opening on it (stake), rewinding, then paying it off.

Generic 75 to 90 s template built from measured techniques (replace the content with the brief's):

| s | Beat | On screen | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 to 3 | Character + stake | a typed request over its world, a spinner, the music stops | typed ask + stop-time |
| 3 to 8 | Hidden questions | one sentence explodes into 4 to 6 question pills | `HiddenQuestions` |
| 8 to 10 | Brand turn | brand-shape window whip-zoom into the product world | `ShapeWindowZoom` |
| 10 to 26 | Act 1, one continuous take | hero card becomes the product panel; macro push to the one value; a button brand-ifies | `CardToPanel`, `UIPlane`, `Brandify` |
| 26 to 36 | Act 2 | a plain-English setting typed, live sliders rewrite it, a caption becomes the input | live sliders, `CaptionToInput`, colour-tail type-on |
| 36 to 48 | Under the hood (the one dark block) | dive into a chip → staircase of checks → one calm HELD → reasoning log → surface back | `ChipDive`, `AuditStaircase`, `ReasoningLog` |
| 48 to 58 | Human in the loop | anchored popover from the button; options ringed 1.05 s each; counted toasts n/N | popover, `OptionRing`, toasts |
| 58 to 64 | Proof | a record composes from skeleton, sweep reveal, hero chip pops: "Approved automatically" next to "Held" | skeleton, sweep, hero chip |
| 64 to 72 | Breadth (optional) | a pinned badge while contexts slide beneath, or a locked diagram over changing plates | pinned carousel, locked diagram |
| 72 to 84 | Line + tag + CTA | breath-and-hit, word-locked line with tracking collapse, stair tagline, lockup, CTA ≥ 4 s | breath-and-hit, `TrackIn` |

### 2.2 Short house film (45 to 65 s)

1. **Hook (0 to 6 s)**: a question or tension line built word by word, then the answer with the old line shifting into a header.
2. **Problem (6 to 30 s)**: accumulate on one canvas (camera, self-drawing hairlines, pills, a ticking counter) plus one proof of risk. Music drives.
3. **Turn (30 to 40 s)**: a light reset, the music breaks down, a typed line, then the name morph or the brand-shape iris.
4. **Answer (40 to 52 s)**: the pillars, one rule card (scan, then ring-check or a held lift-out), the real product in a tilted window. Music drops back in.
5. **Payoff and CTA (52 to 65 s)**: the layer stack, a slot-roll list, the CTA with a marker and the URL.

### 2.3 Product overview / sizzle (45 to 100 s)

- **Claim / proof alternation**: claims of 2 to 5 words on their own 1.5 to 4.5 s dark scene, each followed by a light 5 to 15 s proof. Name reveal at ~20 to 25%. One contrast beat with a music dip. Name said/shown twice (start and end). Product within 6 to 8 s for social.
- **Protagonist narrative**: a business with a goal in the first 5 s, three verb chapters (field → chip cards), each one long canvas take (10 to 27 s) ending in an artefact.
- Measured ratios (79 s): cold claim 5% · beat montage 3% · values 13% · name reveal at 22% · interaction + pipeline 25% · contrast 3% · differentiator 3% · freedoms 18% · deploy/scale 16% · name + endline 9%.
- **Single-object variant**: one hero object, many states, no cuts for ~38 s; warm world → cool world (process) → warm world (payoff) via 12 f gradient sweeps.

### 2.4 Workflow / assistant demo (60 to 100 s)

| Beat | Ratio |
|---|---|
| Title / promise / problem-as-question / stat (benefit stated 3 times before any UI) | ~30% |
| Detect + propose (options ringed 1.05 s each) | 16% |
| Human audits the reasoning and sources (spotlight) | 12% |
| Approve → execute (named sub-steps as status rows) → result number | 16% |
| Recap + CTA | 21% |

Chapter cards (1.7 to 2.7 s) only in music-only VO gaps; backgrounds light-ramp per chapter. Request → result → confirm loops take 10 to 15 s (8 to 10 s if dense). Fix raw captures with push-ins 1.6 to 2.2x, typing speed-ramped 3 to 4x, step pills, agenda ticks between steps.

### 2.5 Explainer (100 to 245 s)

- Problem (4%) → product named (2%) → footage pillars (9%) → **architecture drawn three times, one layer each** (17%) → stats (16%) → demo that instantiates the diagram (39%) → close.
- Category-first variant: familiar frame → "but that's only part" → category name → verbs → a trust hinge at ≤ 40% → governance → product by 40% (not 66%).
- Presenter variant: typed chapter questions as the spine; one metaphor before any UI; the expert on camera for long ideas, b-roll for lists; a visual beat every 6 to 8 s; metaphor callback ~60 s later.

### 2.6 Keynote sizzle and bumpers

~1 shot/s, no words until the final spoken line. One-sentence chapter bumpers of 6 to 10 s: glyph assembles (1 s) → word-slam at 200 to 260 px, 6 to 8 f/word (2 s) → the line scales to a 44 px caption over 2 to 3 cuts (3 s) → pale card (1.5 s). Reprise close replays the opener faster.

### 2.7 Ad spot (13 to 19 s), a modular kit

| Slot | Time | Visual job |
|---|---|---|
| A. Ask or intro | 0 to 2.0 s (11 to 15%) | macro typed prompt with a match cut to a wide, or an identity card |
| B. Product at work | ~3.1 s (20 to 24%), the longest shot | one surface, one transformation (pending → working → done) with an on-surface status pill; one held beat then release |
| C. Two cards | ~1.3 s each | 2 to 5 words each; card 2 ends with a click-squash on its last word, hard cut next frame |
| D. Checklist | 2.6 to 4.3 s (chip variant 0.85 s) | three ✓ lines written by two cursors (human, system) |
| E. End card | 2.5 s | logo build + URL ≥ 48 px from the first frame |

15 s variant: drop slot A and open on the identity card. 13 s: chip checklist. Re-time the music for every cut-down.

### 2.8 Launch ad (30 to 45 s), pick one pattern

- **Kinetic anthem**: hook sentence across 3 to 4 cards 0 to 8% · picture of the hook word 3% · team + one character card 10% · role-pair cards with the music dropping on word one 25% · triad of cards each followed by 1 to 2 s of UI 20% · status-cascade payoff 7% · people/system grid thesis 14% · slogan cards 8% · end 6%. ASL ~1.3 s. With a VO, cards punctuate the VO; strobe dark ⇄ light only through the hook.
- **VO overview**: value readable in the first UI frame; each VO verb gets exactly one UI state (2 to 4 s), crash push or modal between; VO pauses 1.1 to 3.2 s on payoffs. ASL ~1.8 s.
- **User-voice call and response**: the human speaks and types; the product answers in silent pills and artefacts; 5 to 7 pairs of 3 to 5 s; one 15 s continuous UI take; at most one footage gag.
- **One-workflow take**: a from → to caption pair, one 7 to 38 s continuous take from source to result with a persistent HUD, then a sentence ending in the status pill.

### 2.9 Roundup / changelog (75 to 80 s), tutorial (2 to 3 min), webinar shell

- **Roundup**: slot grid from data: intro 6.5 to 7 s · 4 × (card 2.1 to 4 s + demo 7.5 to 20 s) · outro 6 to 10 s; VO starts on the card and runs into the demo; a breath (−37 to −43 dB) at each card change; each demo zooms 1.5 to 2.5x over 15 to 24 f onto the one control and ends on a confirmation toast.
- **Tutorial**: question hook 4 s → answer + name 4 s → title ≤ 3 s → 3-step mantra carousel 4 s → body (a prompt lift per instruction; one deliberate mistake + revert) → tips table → end screen. Shell 13% / body 60% / tips 12% / outro 12%; shell cuts 0.5 to 1.5 s, body takes 3 to 15 s with internal zooms.
- **Webinar shell**: three build-in cards → a short sizzle under the presenter's voice → a second sizzle that settles into the demo frame (12 f scale); always a bed and hits under sizzles; never OS chrome, backup slates or silent bumpers.

### 2.10 Social reel (9:16, 30 to 55 s, sweet spot ~40 s)

```
Hook: the payoff or provocation spoken at 0.0 s, subject named in sentence one, the visual performs it (0 to 5 s)
→ Proof number, spoken AND shown as a counter (by 4 to 7 s)
→ Name (can be late; benefit first)
→ One relatable pain, measured (hours, steps, clicks, money)
→ Mechanism in 3 verbs / 3 steps, ONE example object carried throughout
→ Twist at 45 to 68% of runtime + theme flip
→ Trust stack (free, open, no account); caveats mid-body or in the post caption, never right before the CTA
→ CTA that echoes the pain, single ask
```
- **What decides reach**: topic and hook first, CTA mechanics second; motion is table stakes. Never trade a stronger hook for fancier animation.
- **Topic tests**: a name viewers already know (or a character); a dream outcome or felt pain; usable today; one proof number.
- **Hook rules**: payoff at 0.0 s, proof by 7 s; banned: opening on someone else's product, two product names before any benefit, a feature claim with no tension, insider nouns in line one.
- **Simplify**: one new word per reel, defined in the same breath; every number gets a comparison; at most one number per beat and 3 per reel; "you" framing; abstract ideas become physical actions; plant an open loop by ~6 s and pay it off at ~60%.
- **Visual grammar**: one continuous canvas, objects caused by words (a noun → event cue map: every named item gets its chip, check, toggle, count-up or typed text on its exact word), morphs instead of cuts; chapter chrome for multi-step reels over 35 s; world 1 light with a faint grid, world 2 dark for the problem or twist, flips on a breath.
- **Abstract → literal UI state**: counters/timers for effort or speed, strike or stamp for "not X", scan-line X-ray for "under the hood", tethers for lock-in, a persistent stepper for processes, a highlighter on real text for proof.
- **Layout**: one hero card centred between 35% and 65% of the height; keep the bottom 25% clear for the platform UI.
- **CTA journey (~4 s)**: profile card → Follow tapped → comment sheet types the keyword → "Sent" → a message banner with a link card. Keyword = the outcome word or product noun, never a generic word. Single ask; no caveat in the last 8 s; no conditional CTA.
- **Pre-flight**: topic passes the tests; subject + payoff in sentence one with a performing visual; proof number by 7 s; one new word; ≤ 3 numbers each with a comparison; one example object; twist at 45 to 68% with a flip; 30 to 55 s at 150 to 180 wpm; cue map written; caption boxes ≤ 1 per phrase; music ≥ 10 dB under the voice.

## 3. Devices for systems, assistants and approvals (generic)

| Device | Use |
|---|---|
| Named workers as live status lines ("Checking 4 rules…", one row swap every ~30 f, spinner 1 rev / 24 f) | showing automated work without VO |
| Coordinator → workers fan-out with a tools/rules tray in one frame | architecture of a multi-step system |
| Stepper chain pending → active → done, with a distinct HELD state | linear processes |
| Hub (centre + clockwise dotted spokes) | one thing connected to many |
| Workflow card with options ("Yes / Yes with review / Modify") | the human choice |
| Audit log of actions with state chips (row lift, spotlight) | governance |
| Proposal cards with confidence + approve | choosing among options |
| Generation ticker ("Creating… / Applying… / Done ✓") with the matching card resolving in sync | progress |
| **Reasoning as a verbatim log** (each line ≥ 28 px; ✓ before text; the HOLD line in the deny colour) | auditable reasoning, not a sparkle |
| **Human in the loop**: propose → audit reasoning + sources → approve → execute → record updates | the demo spine |
| Correction that teaches ("done manually · next time automatic") | learning without hype |
| A typed plain-English rule, **then triggered on screen** | rules made tangible |
| **One calm denial in a chain of checks** (no shake, no red flash) | trust |
| Trust as a ruled path straightening a maze; one lit route through a graph | grounding |
| "Approved automatically" next to "Held" as two quiet statuses on the record | the whole product in two lines |
| A receipt whose sections fan open with real proof callouts | proof of control |
| A disclaimer as reassurance under every prompt | honesty |

Visual grammar for people and systems working together: a profile card built name → role → status pill → stamp; circle = person, rounded square = system/agent, equal size on one grid; person cursor ink + circle tag, system cursor accent + square tag; the person speaks aloud, the system speaks only in pills and logs; one persistent HUD per system actor; values move between UIs on a hairline tether.

## 4. Script and VO writing rules

- One claim per sentence; one new noun per clause when a diagram builds.
- Concrete over abstract: name the amount, the counterparty, the rule. Run-on claim lists ("secure integrations… for faster deployment with reliable performance") and abstract buzzwords are anti-patterns; so are "and soon" roadmap claims. Show only what ships; label illustrative demos as such.
- Status micro-copy carries system behaviour; never read UI text aloud.
- Prompts are plain-English imperatives readable in < 2 s.
- Human, specific copy; no AI-sounding slogans; no em or en dashes on screen.
- Write the VO at 125 to 145 wpm, then time it with the builder before storyboarding the frames.
