# Klarna: AI customer service, then humans again — research notes

Researched 2026-10-17. The full claim list with URLs is in `sources.json`.

## Story arc
1. **Feb 27, 2024.** Klarna announced an AI assistant "powered by OpenAI". After one month live globally, Klarna said it had handled 2.3M conversations, which was two-thirds of its customer service chats. Klarna also said it did "the equivalent work of 700 full-time agents" and that customers resolved errands in under 2 minutes, down from 11. (c1–c8)
2. **2024.** Klarna froze most hiring. Headcount fell from 5,527 (end of 2022) to 3,422 (end of 2024), according to the IPO filing as reported by CNBC. The CEO says attrition was part of the reason. (c9, c10)
3. **May 8, 2025, Bloomberg interview.** CEO Sebastian Siemiatkowski said the focus on cost had produced "lower quality". He said customers should always be able to reach a human. Klarna started an "Uber type of setup" pilot with remote in-house agents. (c11–c14)
4. **The same week, Klarna clarified.** A spokesperson said the AI still handles about two-thirds of inquiries. Klarna presented the move as the next step of its AI strategy, not a reversal. (c15)
5. **Sept 10, 2025.** Klarna IPO'd on the NYSE (KLAR) at $40 a share, a valuation of about $15B. (c16)
6. **Nov 2025, Q3 results.** The CEO said the AI now does the work of 853+ agents and has saved $60M, so Klarna is doubling down on AI. (c17)
7. **Feb 2026.** The CEO said human support will become "like the VIP treatment", while AI handles the simplest tasks. (c18)

**The honest takeaway:** Klarna did not abandon AI. It kept the AI on routine chats and repositioned humans as the quality/premium layer.

## Strongest numbers (all Klarna's own claims, unaudited)
- 2.3 million conversations in the first month, or two-thirds of customer service chats. Klarna press release, Feb 27, 2024.
- Equivalent work of 700 full-time agents. Same release.
- Resolution time under 2 minutes, down from 11. Same release.
- 25% drop in repeat inquiries, live in 23 markets, more than 35 languages. Same release.
- $40M estimated profit improvement in 2024. This was an estimate.
- 853+ agents' worth of work and $60M saved. Q3 2025 earnings call, reported by CX Dive on Nov 20, 2025.
- Employees went from 5,527 to 3,422 (Dec 2022 to Dec 2024). IPO prospectus, via CNBC on May 14, 2025. This is the most "fact-like" number.

## Caveats for the script
- **Don't say "fired 700 people."** Seven hundred was an FTE-equivalence claim. Klarna's agents were largely outsourced ("the few thousand human agents" it outsources), and no source documents 700 layoffs.
- **Prefer "hire" over "rehire."** "Rehire" is media framing. Klarna is hiring in-house agents to replace outsourced ones, and the pilot had only two people at first.
- **Don't say Klarna "abandoned AI" or "turned off the bot."** The AI still handled about two-thirds of chats in May 2025, and Klarna touted it as doing 853 agents' worth of work in Nov 2025.
- **The human option already existed in 2024.** The Feb 2024 release said customers "can still choose to interact with live agents". The 2025 change was about quality and in-house staffing, not adding a human option for the first time.
- **Cite Bloomberg carefully.** Bloomberg is paywalled. The quotes were verified via CX Dive, The Independent, CX Today and Entrepreneur citing Bloomberg. Bloomberg's line that the AI push "has gone too far" is a paraphrase, not a quote.
- **Treat the Feb 2026 source with care.** The "VIP" quote is from Charter partner content hosted on TIME, not TIME editorial.
- **One outlet has the wrong share.** Entrepreneur says 75% of chats, but Klarna's own figure is two-thirds. Use two-thirds.
