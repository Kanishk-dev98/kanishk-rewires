## Open questions

- The full text of the Bloomberg 2025-05-08 interview could not be accessed (paywall/403). The quotes were verified only via secondary outlets citing it (CX Dive, The Independent, CX Today, Entrepreneur).
- The F-1 text on sec.gov was not read directly. The headcount figures (5,527 -> 3,422) come from CNBC's reading of the prospectus. I did not verify whether the F-1 repeats the 700-agent or two-thirds claims.
- How many in-house human agents Klarna eventually hired under the 'Uber-type' pilot is unknown. The only number found is two (May 2025).
- No independent or audited verification exists of the 700/853/850 FTE-equivalence, the 11->2 minute resolution time, CSAT parity, the $40M estimate or the $60M savings.
- Whether the 2024 '700 agents' corresponded to actual job losses is unclear. Klarna's agents were largely outsourced, and no source documents 700 layoffs.
- Whether there was any Klarna statement after Feb 2026 (through Oct 2026) on AI customer service; none was found in this search pass.
- The 'two-thirds' share (Klarna) vs '75%' (Entrepreneur) discrepancy: Klarna's primary wording is two-thirds.
