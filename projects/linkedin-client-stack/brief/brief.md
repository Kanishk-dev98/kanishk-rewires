# Creative brief: the 5-layer LinkedIn client acquisition stack

**Audience.** Freelancers, consultants, agency owners and B2B founders who want clients from LinkedIn and are willing to set up a system.

**Core promise.** A practical blueprint: an optimized profile, a qualified prospect list, personalized outreach, follow-up tracking and booked calls.

**Format decision (from the user's feedback on the Klarna sample).** This is a motion-graphics tutorial, not a typography video. Every layer shows its setup step happening in an illustrative interface: the headline gets rewritten, ICP filters fill in, leads get prioritized by AI, research becomes a draft that a human approves, a workflow wires itself up, and a CRM card moves to Booked. The camera tilts, zooms and follows the cursor. The brand is small: a corner mark during the video and a compact outro.

**Tension.** People spray generic connection requests and either get ignored or reach for automation tools that LinkedIn bans. The system shows a better way: the human does the outreach and the automation handles everything around it.

**Progression.** Hook (profile becomes a five-step system) → 01 profile → 02 prospects → 03 personalize → 04 automate → 05 convert → the five layers link into one ring that becomes the K·R monogram.

**Factual guardrails (research/notes.md).**
- LinkedIn's User Agreement (section 8.2) bans bots and automated messaging. Outreach is always shown as a manual, human-approved step, and automation only touches the CRM, tasks and booking.
- Sales Navigator lead filters (industry, title, headcount, geography) and Apollo search filters are verified. Exports depend on the plan.
- Connection notes are capped at 200 characters. The example note is 146.
- Make and n8n are shown only as CRM and task workflows (both have HubSpot and Pipedrive integrations), never as LinkedIn senders.
- No conversion rates and no promised results. All people, companies and counts are illustrative and labelled as such.

**Format.** 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps, exactly 40 s (36 s body plus a 4 s outro), with the narration as supplied.
