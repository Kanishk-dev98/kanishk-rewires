# Source ledger: Building a LinkedIn client acquisition system (Sales Navigator / Apollo -> Make or n8n -> CRM -> booking link): fact check for a 40-second educational Instagram reel

Researched: 2026-10-17

- **c1** (fact, event User Agreement effective 2025-11-03 (as displayed on 2026-10-17)): LinkedIn's User Agreement and Help Center prohibit third-party software, bots, crawlers, browser plug-ins/extensions that scrape LinkedIn or automate activity on it, including automated adding of contacts and sending of messages.
  - LinkedIn, "User Agreement", published Effective November 3, 2025: https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement
  - LinkedIn Help, "Prohibited software and extensions", published undated: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387
  - Note: User Agreement section 8.2 (Don'ts): you agree that you will not "Develop, support or use software, devices, scripts, robots or any other means or processes (such as crawlers, browser plugins and add-ons or any other technology) to scrape or copy the Services..." and will not "Use bots or other unauthorized automated methods to access the Services, add or download contacts, send or redirect messages, create, comment on, like, share, or re-share posts, or otherwise drive inauthentic engagement." Help page a1341387: "we don't permit the use of any third party software, including \"crawlers\", bots, browser plug-ins, or browser extensions ... that scrape, modify the appearance of, or automate activity on LinkedIn's website." Quotes obtained via a fetch tool that may have trimmed text between fragments; the wording of each fragment is as returned. Professional Community Policies page was not separately checked.
- **c2** (fact, event as of 2026-10-17): LinkedIn Sales Navigator lead search can be filtered by Industry, Current job title, Function, Seniority level, Company headcount and Geography (region or postal code), among other filters.
  - LinkedIn Help, "Sales Navigator lead and account filter definitions", published undated: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a1452817
  - LinkedIn Help, "Filter your search results in Sales Navigator", published undated: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a101031
  - Note: Definitions quoted on a1452817: Industry: "Filter by industry to identify leads in your target industries. For example, Manufacturing." Current job title: "Filter by the job titles you are targeting of leads' current roles." Seniority level: "Filter by seniority level to find leads matching your ideal customer profile." Company headcount: "Filter leads by the number of employees at a company." Geography: "Filter by region, country, state/province, city or postal code to identify leads". Function: "Filter leads by their job function category." Sales Navigator is a paid product; some filters (e.g. buyer intent, past customer) depend on plan tier. a101031 was found via search but not fetched.
- **c3** (company_claim, event as of 2026-10-17): Apollo.io lets you search people with filters including job title, industry, company employee count and location, and save/export contacts (export limits depend on plan).
  - Apollo.io developer docs, "People API Search", published undated: https://docs.apollo.io/reference/people-api-search
  - Apollo.io, "B2B Contact Database", published undated: https://www.apollo.io/product/search
  - Apollo Knowledge Base, "Use Search Filters to Find Prospects", published undated: https://knowledge.apollo.io/hc/en-us/articles/4412665755661-Use-Search-Filters-to-Find-Prospects
  - Note: Verified directly from the API reference: person_titles[] ("Job titles held by the people you want to find"), person_locations[] ("The location where people live"), organization_num_employees_ranges[] ("The number range of employees working for the person's current company"), industry via industry_tag_ids[] / organization_industries[]. Product page: "Apollo's 65+ filters..." and "A bi-directional sync with Salesforce, HubSpot, and Pipedrive". The Knowledge Base articles returned HTTP 403 to direct fetch; the in-app filter names (Job titles, # of employees, Location) and export details (CSV export; free plan can select up to 25 records at a time for export) come only from a search-engine summary of those KB pages and were NOT directly verified. Treat plan-specific export limits as unverified.
- **c4** (fact, event as of 2026-10-17): Make and n8n both have official HubSpot and Pipedrive integrations that can create/update contacts/persons and deals and create tasks/activities; neither offers an official LinkedIn module/node that sends connection requests or DMs. Their official LinkedIn integrations are limited to posting (and, in Make, company post/statistics retrieval).
  - n8n Docs, "LinkedIn node documentation", published undated: https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/app-nodes/n8n-nodes-base.linkedin/
  - Make, "LinkedIn (Make app documentation)", published undated: https://apps.make.com/linkedin
  - n8n Docs, "HubSpot node documentation", published undated: https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/app-nodes/n8n-nodes-base.hubspot/
  - n8n Docs, "Pipedrive node documentation", published undated: https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/app-nodes/n8n-nodes-base.pipedrive/
  - Make, "HubSpot CRM (Make app documentation)", published undated: https://apps.make.com/hubspotcrm
  - Make, "Pipedrive (Make app documentation)", published undated: https://apps.make.com/pipedrive
  - Note: n8n LinkedIn node: one resource/operation, Post > Create ("n8n supports creating posts"); docs suggest HTTP Request node for unsupported operations. Make LinkedIn modules: Create a User Text/Image/Video Post, Delete a User Post, company post create/list/get/delete/watch, follower and share statistics, Make an API Call. No connection-request or messaging module in either. Make HubSpot CRM: Create or Update a Contact, Create a Deal, Update a Deal, Create a Task, Update a Task, etc. Make Pipedrive: Create/Update a Person, Create/Update a Deal, Create/Update an Activity. n8n HubSpot: Contact 'Create/Update a contact', Deal 'Create a deal'/'Update a deal', Engagement 'Create an engagement' (the n8n page does not use the word 'task'; HubSpot engagements include tasks but this was not confirmed on the n8n page). n8n Pipedrive: Person create/update, Deal create/update, Activity create/update. Generic 'Make an API Call'/HTTP Request can hit LinkedIn's API, but LinkedIn's public API does not offer member connection/messaging to general developers (not separately verified here). Community nodes / third-party apps not covered.
- **c5** (fact, event as of 2026-10-17): A personalized note on a LinkedIn connection request can be up to 200 characters; members without Premium can add a personalized note to a limited number of requests per month (LinkedIn Help currently states three), while Premium members can personalize all invitations.
  - LinkedIn Help, "Personalize invitations to connect", published undated: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a563153
  - Note: Quoted: "Each message can be up to 200 characters." and "You can add a personalized message to up to three connection requests per month." and "LinkedIn Premium members can send unlimited personalized invitations to all their connection requests (connection request limits may still apply)." CONFLICT: a search-engine summary of the same page said 'up to five per month'; the direct fetch on 2026-10-17 returned 'three'. Third-party posts report 5-15/month and variance by account, suggesting LinkedIn tests this. Reel should say 'a few per month on free accounts' or cite the 200-char cap only. Older reports of a 300-char limit are outdated.
- **c6** (fact, event as of 2026-10-17): Calendly and Cal.com provide booking links and have native HubSpot integrations that sync bookings into HubSpot (contact creation and meeting activity/records).
  - Calendly Help Center, "Calendly + HubSpot", published undated: https://calendly.com/help/calendly-hubspot
  - Cal.com Help, "HubSpot and Cal.com", published undated: https://cal.com/help/apps-and-integrations/hubspot
  - Note: Calendly: "If the invitee's email doesn't exist in HubSpot, a new contact is created." "If the contact already exists in HubSpot, a meeting activity is added without changing the contact's existing details." Available on Professional, Standard, Standard Plus, Teams, Teams Plus, Enterprise plans (not free); requires Calendly owner/admin and HubSpot admin; works with free or paid HubSpot. Cal.com: "On booking, we create a meeting record in HubSpot and associate it with the attendee's contact record"; reschedule updates dates and sets outcome "Rescheduled"; cancellation sets outcome "Canceled"; option to skip creating new contacts. Cal.com plan requirements for the HubSpot app not checked.
- **c7** (interpretation, event User Agreement effective 2025-11-03): The LinkedIn User Agreement explicitly lists 'add or download contacts, send or redirect messages' via bots or unauthorized automated methods as prohibited, so auto-sending connection requests or DMs from a personal profile with third-party tools violates LinkedIn's terms.
  - LinkedIn, "User Agreement", published Effective November 3, 2025: https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement
  - Note: Interpretation of section 8.2 applied to connection-request/DM automation tools. LinkedIn may restrict accounts that violate terms; specific enforcement outcomes not verified here.
