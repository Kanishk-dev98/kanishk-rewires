**Sound:** Clicks on the edit and on the CTA.

**Transition:** rise

**Visual concept:** Lead search: ICP filter chips fill in, five illustrative leads populate with fit scores and signals, then an AI prioritize click re-sorts them and tags priority.

**Animation:** Camera starts on the filters, moves to the list, and pushes in as rows re-sort.

**Sound:** Clicks on filters and on AI prioritize.

**Transition:** rise

- Source c2: LinkedIn Sales Navigator lead search can be filtered by Industry, Current job title, Function, Seniority level, Company headcount and Geography (region or postal code), among other filters. (fact) LinkedIn Help, undated https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a1452817
- Source c3: Apollo.io lets you search people with filters including job title, industry, company employee count and location, and save/export contacts (export limits depend on plan). (company_claim) Apollo.io developer docs, undated https://docs.apollo.io/reference/people-api-search

## 4. s04 · personalize · 5.8 s

**Voiceover:** Use AI to turn relevant research into personalized messages. Review each one before it goes out.

**On screen:** Research → AI draft → Human review / Sarah Chen / Head of Growth · Northwind Goods / Company news: expanding its D2C range / Hiring a CRM manager / Spoke on a retail podcast / Hi, I'd love to connect and explore synergies. / Hi Sarah, noticed your team is expanding its D2C range. I work on retention systems for growing brands and thought it would be useful to connect.

**Visual concept:** Prospect record: one genuine business detail is picked, the generic note is struck out, AI types a contextual draft with a character counter, and a human approves it.

**Animation:** Camera moves down the record as each step happens; the cursor picks, edits and approves.

**Sound:** Clicks on the pick and the approve.

**Transition:** rise

- Source c5: A personalized note on a LinkedIn connection request can be up to 200 characters; members without Premium can add a personalized note to a limited number of requests per month (LinkedIn Help currently states three), while Premium members can personalize all invitations. (fact) LinkedIn Help, undated https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a563153

## 5. s05 · workflow · 6.9 s

**Voiceover:** Connect your CRM to an automation workflow. Track replies, schedule follow-up tasks, and stop when someone responds.

**On screen:** CRM + reminders + reply tracking / Make or n8n · illustrative / Prospect added to CRM / CRM / Create reminder task / TASK / Human sends approved outreach / YOU / Check reply status / IF / No reply: follow-up task / TASK / Replied: stop sequence / STOP / Contacted · next: reminder / No reply · follow-up due Thu / Replied · sequence stopped

**Visual concept:** A workflow builder wires itself: trigger, task, a manual human step, a reply check that branches to follow-up or stop. A CRM record updates underneath.

**Animation:** Nodes pop in and edges draw on the spoken words; a lime pulse travels the chain; the camera follows each branch.

**Sound:** Soft ticks as nodes connect, a switch on stop.

**Transition:** rise

- Source c1: LinkedIn's User Agreement and Help Center prohibit third-party software, bots, crawlers, browser plug-ins/extensions that scrape LinkedIn or automate activity on it, including automated adding of contacts and sending of messages. (fact) LinkedIn, Effective November 3, 2025 https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement
- Source c4: Make and n8n both have official HubSpot and Pipedrive integrations that can create/update contacts/persons and deals and create tasks/activities; neither offers an official LinkedIn module/node that sends connection requests or DMs. Their official LinkedIn integrations are limited to posting (and, in Make, company post/statistics retrieval). (fact) n8n Docs, undated https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/app-nodes/n8n-nodes-base.linkedin/
