# The decoy effect

- **Category:** business.
- **Format:** 9:16 cinematic concept explainer, about 50 s. No step numbers. On-screen text is data and labels only; captions carry the words.
- **Visual spine:** 100 dots (one per student) flow into three price cards. The decoy is removed and they flow back.
- **Honesty:** the Economist case is "a classroom demo Ariely describes". The 1982 Huber, Payne & Puto paper is credited as the origin. The popcorn menu is labelled illustrative. The real-world effect is shown as about 1% (3.6M wine purchases). The how-to says the decoy must be a real offer.
- **Caption caveat:** hypothetical classroom choices; replications are mixed.
