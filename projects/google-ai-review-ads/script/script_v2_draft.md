# Google is writing part of your ads — script v2 (DRAFT, waiting for approval)

Why v2: the user said a layman doesn't get the point at the start, text collides with the phone, the phone is
everywhere, and the video doesn't use the 3D and effects skills. v2 opens from the searcher's point of view (everyone
has seen a Google ad), gives each beat its own visual system, and uses the phone once.

Length: about 42 s of voice (about 118 words) plus the 2.6 s brand ending.
Layout rule: the top 380 px are reserved for the headline captions; no object ever enters that zone.
