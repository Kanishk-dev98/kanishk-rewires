# Google now writes part of your ad

Status: DRAFT v1, waiting for the user's approval
Format: 9x16, 1080x1920, 30 fps
Length: about 42 s of voice plus the 3 s K·R brand ending (about 45 s total)
Voice: about 120 words at about 170 words a minute
Structure: news development → what it means → twist → practical takeaway
One new word: "review summary" (defined in the same breath)
One example object, carried throughout: one ad card for a bakery (an illustration, labelled "Example"; not a real business, not a copy of Google's interface)
Numbers in the video: one ("4 countries")

## Scene by scene

### 1. Hook · 0.0 to 4.0 s
**Voiceover:** Google just started writing part of your ads. And you can't edit it.
**On screen:** GOOGLE IS WRITING / YOUR ADS
**Visual:** A blank ad card on cream. A line of text types itself into the card on its own; a cursor reaches for it and the line locks (small lock icon).
**Sound:** soft keyboard ticks under the typing, a muted click on the lock.
**Source:** c1, c5

### 2. Proof · 4.0 to 8.5 s
**Voiceover:** It's live in four countries. The US, UK, Australia, and India.
**On screen:** 4 COUNTRIES, with India highlighted in lime
**Visual:** Four location chips stack up; the India chip lands last and lights lime.
**Source:** c3

### 3. What it is · 8.5 to 15.0 s
**Voiceover:** It's called a review summary. Google's AI reads your reviews across the web, and writes one line into your ad.
**On screen:** REVIEW SUMMARY (small definition: AI-written, from your reviews)
**Visual:** Review cards from around the web fly in, compress into one line, and that line drops into the ad card.
**Source:** c1, c2

### 4. The pain · 15.0 to 24.0 s
**Voiceover:** Picture your bakery's ad. You wrote the headline. Google writes the line inside it. You can't change a word, and Google hasn't said how to switch it off.
**On screen:** YOU: THE HEADLINE / GOOGLE: THE REST
**Visual:** The bakery ad card, labelled "Example". The headline is cream; the AI line is outlined in lime with the lock. A cursor tries to click it and nothing happens.
**Source:** c5, c7

### 5. Twist (theme flips from cream to charcoal) · 24.0 to 31.5 s
**Voiceover:** Here's the part that got me. It's positive only. And it shows up only when Google predicts your ad will do better.
**On screen:** POSITIVE ONLY / SHOWN WHEN IT PERFORMS
**Visual:** Lights drop to charcoal. The review cards return; the negative ones fall away out of frame, only the positive ones feed the line.
**Source:** c4, c6

### 6. What it means · 31.5 to 35.0 s
**Voiceover:** So your reviews are now your ad copy.
**On screen:** YOUR REVIEWS = YOUR AD COPY
**Visual:** The review cards and the ad card merge into one object.
**Source:** c8 (interpretation)

### 7. Takeaway · 35.0 to 41.5 s
**Voiceover:** So stop polishing headlines. Ask happy customers to say exactly what they loved, because that's what Google writes from.
**On screen:** ASK FOR SPECIFIC REVIEWS
**Visual:** A new review card types itself with a specific line ("the sourdough is still warm at 8 am"), and the ad line updates to match.
**Source:** c8 (interpretation)

### 8. Ending · 41.5 to 45.0 s
**Voiceover:** Follow Kanishk.Rewires. Rethink how you think.
**Visual:** Lines converge into the K·R monogram, then the KANISHK.REWIRES wordmark.
