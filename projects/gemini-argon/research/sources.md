# Source ledger: Google Gemini 4 Argon (consumer + API launch, chat-to-tasks toggle, connected apps)

Researched: 2026-10-18

- **c1** (fact, event n/a): Google calls Gemini 4 Argon "our new frontier model", positioned as "our frontier model for real-world coding, enterprise knowledge work, and cyber defense." Announced Sep 30, 2026 by Koray Kavukcuoglu (SVP, Google DeepMind; Chief AI Architect). Name meaning ('Argon') is not explained in any Google source checked.
  - Google (The Keyword, blog.google) - Koray Kavukcuoglu, "Gemini 4 Argon: our next era of frontier intelligence", published 2026-09-30: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-argon/
- **c2** (fact, event n/a): Model card: Argon is the next generation of Gemini natively multimodal reasoning models, "is not a modification or a fine-tune of a prior model"; sparse mixture-of-experts transformer; text/image/audio/video in, text out; up to 1M-token combined context; knowledge cutoff March 2026. Distributed via Gemini app, Gemini API, Google AI Studio, Gemini for business, Google Antigravity and Google AI Mode.
  - Google DeepMind, "Gemini 4 Argon - Model Card", published 2026-10-13: https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/gemini-4-argon/
