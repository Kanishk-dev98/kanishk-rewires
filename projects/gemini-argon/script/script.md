## 4. s03 · gTask · 7.3 s

**Voiceover:** Here's the shift. Flip the toggle to Task, and hand it a job. Then close your laptop. Gemini keeps working.

**On screen:** Every Monday at 9, sort my inbox, summarize client emails and draft replies / ⏰ Every Mon 9:00 / ✉ Gmail / Monday inbox sweep / SCHEDULED / 31 emails sorted / 6 client summaries / 4 replies drafted

**Visual concept:** Inside a laptop, the Gemini box flips to Task, a scheduled prompt types and submits; the lid closes and a running task card ticks results.

**Transition:** cut

- Source c13: Official wording: Gemini app post describes "a simple toggle from chat to tasks" in the main chat window; "When you choose tasks, Gemini can work for you even when you're not at your computer." Help Center names it the "Chat and Task toggle next to the prompt text box" (feature formerly 'Gemini Spark', renamed 'Task'). (fact) Google (The Keyword, blog.google), 2026-10-13 https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/gemini-4-argon-availability/
- Source c14: Steps (web/Mac): go to gemini.google.com or open the Gemini Mac app -> click "Task" below the text box -> describe what you want (optionally say when it should run, type @ to pick a skill, ask for generated files, pick a model/thinking level from the model menu; add files via Upload & tools) -> click Submit and monitor; Gemini may ask you to take over its browser for some steps. (fact) Google (Gemini Apps Help), accessed 2026-10-18 https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094507?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop

Turn off via Settings > Task settings > 'Use task mode to hand off work' > Turn off (schedules pause, nothing deleted). (caveat) Google (Gemini Apps Help), accessed 2026-10-18 https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094507?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop
