# Creative brief: the flip that sold a 2028 car

**Format.** A cinematic newsjacking reel, a new format separate from the 7-step tutorials. It runs about 35 s at 9:16, with letterbox, 3D hero shots, graded licensed footage and premium motion graphics.

**What we sell.** The marketing insight, not the news. The Roadster reveal (Oct 15, 2026, at a SpaceX test site) is the hook. Musk's playbook is the payoff: he stages moments instead of selling specs, he turns delays into anticipation, and he lets earned media do the advertising right before earnings.

**Visuals.**
- The car is a CGI recreation of a generic concept car (Khronos CarConcept, CC BY 4.0, credited), with its third-party logos hidden and labelled "visualization". We use no Tesla footage, which avoids copyright claims.
- The lesson scene uses licensed Pexels crowd footage, graded to charcoal and lime.
- The viral scene is pure motion graphics.

**Facts.** All are in `research/sources.json`. The speed figures are labelled as Tesla's claims. "The press did the advertising" is framed as earned media, not as a claim that Tesla buys no ads.

**Ending.** The small lowercase kanishk.rewires, with "Follow for the strategy behind the headlines".
