# 10 prompts for ChatGPT in Chrome
From kanishk.rewires. Setup: install the ChatGPT extension (publisher: OpenAI). In the ChatGPT desktop app, go to Settings, then Computer Use, and connect Chrome. Type @Chrome in your chat.

## Research (Chat mode)
1. **Compare anything.** @Chrome compare the products in my open tabs on price, warranty, delivery time and return policy. Put it in one table and tell me the best value and why.
2. **Summarize before you read.** @Chrome summarize this article in 5 bullet points, then list 3 claims I should double check.
3. **Competitor scan.** @Chrome I have 4 competitor homepages open. For each, give me their headline, who they target, their main offer and their pricing. Then tell me one gap none of them cover.
4. **Video to notes.** @Chrome summarize this YouTube video from its transcript. Give me the key steps and the timestamps where each one starts.

## Shopping and money (Chat mode)
5. **Price check.** @Chrome is this the same product on every open tab? If so, which seller is cheapest after shipping and tax?
6. **Review filter.** @Chrome read the reviews on this page and tell me the 3 most common complaints and how many reviews mention each one.

## Admin (Work mode, review before you submit)
7. **Forms from notes.** @Chrome fill this registration form using these details: [paste details]. Stop before the final submit so I can review it.
8. **Tab cleanup.** @Chrome group my open tabs by topic, name each group and close any duplicates.
9. **Download sweep.** @Chrome download every PDF linked on this page into one folder and list the file names.
10. **Data entry.** @Chrome copy each row from this sheet into the form on the other tab, one entry at a time. Pause after the first one so I can check it.

## Stay safe
- Close banking, payroll and other sensitive tabs first.
- When ChatGPT asks to use a new site, choose "Allow once".
- Always press the final submit, send or pay button yourself.
