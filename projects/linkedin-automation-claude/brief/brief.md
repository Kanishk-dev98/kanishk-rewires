# Creative brief: automate LinkedIn lead generation and posting with Claude Code

**Goal.** Beat the reference reel (ref-1000319940) on its own grammar. Every step shows the real setup action inside realistic product interfaces: Claude Code's terminal, an Apollo people search, LinkedIn's actual "Add a note to your invitation" box with its 200 character counter, a HubSpot pipeline, a LinkedIn post and a Publora week calendar. Each is in the product's real colours and framed in our charcoal, cream and lime theme.

**What changed from the first LinkedIn reel (user feedback).** The script now explains how to build the automation, not what the layers are. The interfaces are realistic, with official logos and licensed headshots, not typography screenshots. The voice runs at a natural pace. The wordmark is small, lowercase and set entirely in IBM Plex Mono.

**Story.** Hook: automate LinkedIn leads with Claude Code. "So simple. Only 7 steps." Then: 1 connect Apollo, HubSpot and Publora; 2 write ICP.md once; 3 Claude pulls 50 matching leads from Apollo; 4 Claude scores them and finds one real reason to reach out; 5 Claude drafts the note and you send it yourself; 6 every lead lands in HubSpot with a follow-up task; 7 Claude writes the posts and Publora schedules them. Then Save, then "Comment LINKEDIN".

**Guardrails.**
- LinkedIn bans automated messaging (User Agreement 8.2), so sending is always a human click, and the video says so.
- Posting is scheduled through Publora, a scheduling tool (see research for how LinkedIn's API allows this).
- People, companies and numbers are illustrative.
- The headshots are Unsplash-licensed (PROVENANCE.json).
- The note fits the 200 character limit.
