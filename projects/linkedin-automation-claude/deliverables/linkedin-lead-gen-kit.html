Automate your LinkedIn lead gen with Claude Code

Seven steps: connect three tools, describe your ideal client once, then let Claude find, score and research leads, draft your notes, log everything in your CRM and schedule your posts. You stay the one who presses Send.

Before you start

LinkedIn bans bots and automated messaging (User Agreement, section 8.2). Claude drafts; you review and send every note yourself. Posting through a scheduler that uses LinkedIn's official API is fine.

Connection notes are capped at 200 characters, and free accounts get only a few personalized notes a month.

Apollo searches can use credits on your plan, and HubSpot actions follow your HubSpot permissions.

1

Connect your tools

Run these in your terminal. They add the official Apollo, HubSpot and Publora servers to Claude Code.

terminal
claude mcp add --transport http apollo https://mcp.apollo.io/mcp
claude mcp add --transport http hubspot https://mcp.hubspot.com
claude mcp add --transport http --scope user publora https://mcp.publora.com/mcp

Then open Claude Code, type /mcp, pick each server and choose Authenticate to sign in. A checkmark means it's connected.

2

Write your ideal client once

Save this as ICP.md in your project folder and fill in your own values. Every later prompt reads it.

ICP.md
# Ideal client
industry: consumer goods, D2C
role: Head of Growth, CMO, VP Marketing
company size: 11 to 200 employees
region: US, UK
signal: launching a product, hiring for growth or CRM, raised recently

# What I offer
one line: I help consumer brands turn first orders into repeat buyers.
proof: (a result you can share)
call to action: a 20 minute call
3

Find your leads

prompt
Read ICP.md. Using the Apollo tools, find 50 people who match it.
Save name, title, company, LinkedIn URL, location and one recent company signal to leads.csv.
Stop after 50 results, and tell me how many credits you used.
4

Score and research

prompt
Score every lead in leads.csv from 0 to 100 against ICP.md:
role 40, company size 20, region 10, signal 30.
For each lead, add one real reason to reach out and where you found it.
Sort by score and show me the top 10 as a table.
5

Draft the outreach

prompt
For the top 10 leads, draft a LinkedIn connection note for each.
Under 200 characters. Mention their signal, say what I do in one line, no pitch, no links.
Write them to notes.md with the person's name and profile URL above each note.
Do not send anything. I will review and send them myself.

Open each profile, paste the note, edit it so it sounds like you, and press Send yourself.

6

Track in your CRM

prompt
Using the HubSpot tools, create a contact for each of the top 10 leads.
Add their score and reason to reach out as a note on the contact.
Create a follow-up task for each one, due 3 business days from today.
7

Post on autopilot

prompt
Read wins.md (my notes on results, lessons and client stories).
Write 5 LinkedIn posts in my voice: short lines, one idea each, a clear first line, no hashtag lists.
Show me all 5 and wait for my changes.
After I approve, use the Publora tools to schedule them Monday to Friday at 9:00 AM my time.
