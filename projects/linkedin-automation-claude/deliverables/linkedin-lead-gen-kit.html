Automate your LinkedIn lead gen with Claude Code

Claude finds people who fit your ideal client, scores them, researches one real reason to reach out, drafts your note, logs every lead in your CRM and keeps your posting calendar full. You review, you press Send, you take the calls.

Claude talks to Apollo, HubSpot and Publora through their official MCP servers. The only thing it never does is message people on LinkedIn for you.

You send the messages

LinkedIn's User Agreement (section 8.2) bans bots and automated messaging. Claude drafts; you send. It also keeps your account safe.

200 characters per note

That is LinkedIn's limit for a connection note. Free accounts only get a few personalized notes a month, so spend them on your best fits.

Credits and permissions

Apollo searches can use credits on your plan. HubSpot actions follow your HubSpot permissions. Check both before you run big batches.

What you need
  • Claude Code, on a Claude plan that includes it or with API credits

  • A HubSpot account (the free CRM works)
  • A Publora account with your LinkedIn profile connected
  • A project folder for this, for example linkedin-leads

01
about 10 min, once

Connect your tools

Open a terminal in your project folder and add the three official servers. This gives Claude Code safe, scoped access to each tool through your own login.

terminal
claude mcp add --transport http apollo https://mcp.apollo.io/mcp
claude mcp add --transport http hubspot https://mcp.hubspot.com
claude mcp add --transport http --scope user publora https://mcp.publora.com/mcp

Now start Claude Code with claude, type /mcp, pick each server and choose Authenticate. Your browser opens; sign in and approve. A checkmark next to each one means you're connected.

TIPConnect Publora to your LinkedIn profile inside Publora first, so Claude can see it as a channel when you schedule posts in step 7.

Write your ideal client once

Save this as ICP.md in your project folder and fill in your own values. Every prompt that follows reads this file, so the more specific it is, the better your leads.

ICP.md
# Ideal client
industry: consumer goods, D2C
role: Head of Growth, CMO, VP Marketing
company size: 11 to 200 employees
region: US, UK
signal: launching a product, hiring for growth or CRM, raised recently

# What I offer
one line: I help consumer brands turn first orders into repeat buyers.
proof: (one result you can share, with a number)
call to action: a 20 minute call

Why the signal line matters most: a signal is a reason this person needs you right now. It turns a cold note into a relevant one, and it carries the most weight when Claude scores leads in step 4.

Draft the outreach

Claude writes a short note for each of your top leads. You open each profile, paste, adjust it so it sounds like you, and press Send yourself.

prompt
For the top 10 leads, draft a LinkedIn connection note for each.
Under 200 characters. Mention their signal, say what I do in one line, no pitch, no links.
Write them to notes.md with the person's name and profile URL above each note.
Do not send anything. I will review and send them myself.

What a good note looks like: one detail about them, one line about you, and an easy reason to connect. No pitch and no link in the first message.

Post on autopilot

Keep a simple wins.md file with results, lessons and client stories. Once a week, Claude turns it into five posts in your voice, and after you approve them, Publora schedules them through LinkedIn's official API.

prompt
Read wins.md (my notes on results, lessons and client stories).
Write 5 LinkedIn posts in my voice: short lines, one idea each, a clear first line, no hashtag lists.
Show me all 5 and wait for my changes.
After I approve, use the Publora tools to schedule them Monday to Friday at 9:00 AM my time.

Why this matters for lead gen: when someone accepts your note and visits your profile, a week of useful posts is what makes them reply.

Questions

Before you start

Is this allowed by LinkedIn?

Yes, as set up here. Claude never touches your LinkedIn messages; you send every note yourself. Scheduling posts through Publora uses LinkedIn's official API. What LinkedIn prohibits is bots and tools that automate messaging, connection requests or scraping, and nothing in this setup does that.

Do I need Sales Navigator?

No. Apollo handles the search and filtering here. If you already use Sales Navigator, you can keep using it to view profiles before you send.

Can I use a different CRM?

Yes, if it has an MCP server Claude Code can connect to. This guide uses HubSpot because its official server can create contacts, notes and tasks.

Why not let Claude send the messages too?

Because it breaks LinkedIn's rules and puts your account at risk, and because the personal touch is the part that gets replies. Let Claude do the slow research and drafting; keep the human moment for yourself.

What does it cost?

It depends on your plans. Claude Code needs a Claude plan that includes it or API credits, Apollo searches may use credits, and HubSpot and Publora have free or paid tiers. Check each tool's pricing page before you scale up.
