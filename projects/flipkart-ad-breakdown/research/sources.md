The film carries that into TV shopping: go a size bigger on the screen without a proportionally bigger bill. Coverage describes the tone as humour plus nostalgia, with the film amplified across digital platforms.

Christensen's milkshake researcher "spent a full day in one of the chain's restaurants" and then "interviewed customers who left with milkshake in hand," finding they "faced a long, boring commute."

- **framework-nostalgia** (framework, event n/a): Research in the Journal of Consumer Research ('Nostalgia Weakens the Desire for Money', Lasaleta, Sedikides and Vohs, 2014, vol. 41 no. 3) found across six experiments that nostalgia reduced people's desire for money and made them willing to pay more for products, through a sense of social connectedness. It is a lab study on willingness to pay, not a test of this ad.
