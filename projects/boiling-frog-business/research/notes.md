## Exact usable lines (with attribution)
- "If you put a frog in boiling water, it won't jump out. It will die. If you put it in cold water, it will jump before it gets hot." (Doug Melton, Harvard biologist, *Fast Company*, 1995)
- "The legend is entirely incorrect!" (Victor Hutchison, University of Oklahoma zoologist, quoted by Whit Gibbons)
- "If a frog had a means of getting out, it certainly would get out." (George Zug, Smithsonian)
- "Kodak invented the world's first digital camera." (Kodak milestones, 1975 entry)
- "In 2000, the two tried to sell their startup to Blockbuster for $50 million. They were flat-out rejected." (*Fortune*, 2023)
- Dates: Kodak filed for Chapter 11 on **Jan 19, 2012**, and Blockbuster on **Sep 23, 2010** (both from SEC 8-Ks).

## Caveats (must follow)
1. **Never say real frogs boil to death.** Present it as a fable or metaphor: "the fable says", then "biologists say real frogs try to escape". The 1800s experiments are mixed, since very slow heating in one 1872 study gave no escape attempts. So don't say "science proved".
2. **Melton's line is about boiling water.** A frog dropped into boiling water is scalded and doesn't jump out. That contradicts the first half of the fable, so don't put "frogs jump out of boiling water" in a scientist's mouth. The hook line is the fable's claim.
3. **Kodak:** "built the first digital camera in 1975" is verified. "Kodak ignored it / buried it" is interpretation, so phrase it as "and still got burned by digital". Kodak emerged from bankruptcy in 2013 and still exists, so say "filed for bankruptcy", not "died".
4. **Netflix/Blockbuster:** the $50M offer comes from the founders' own account. Antioco disputes details. Say "Blockbuster passed" and don't present "laughed at" as fact. If it's used, attribute it: "Netflix's co-founder says they were laughed out of the room."
5. **AI stat (optional):** it must say "in one study" and "writing tasks". Source: Noy & Zhang, *Science* 2023, with a 40% time cut and an 18% quality gain. Real-world gains are likely smaller.
6. **Footage:** none of the clips shows a frog in a pot or in danger. Keep it that way, with no frog and pot composites that imply animal harm.
