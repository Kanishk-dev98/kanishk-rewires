# The boiling frog effect, in business

- **Format:** 9:16 reel, about 45 s, Kanishk.Rewires business explainer.
- **Promise:** Explain the boiling frog effect, show how it kills companies (Kodak, Blockbuster), and teach one action: set your jump line.
- **Structure (per Alok):**
  1. Hook.
  2. The fable: in hot water the frog jumps; heated slowly, it stays until it's too late.
  3. The same thing happens in business.
  4. Proof.
  5. Today.
  6. The action.
  7. Close.
- **Visual grammar:**
  - Real graded frog and heat footage (Pexels and Pixabay, see `studio/public/media/frog/PROVENANCE.json`).
  - A live thermometer that climbs as the narration warms.
  - A creeping KPI dashboard, then-and-collapse timelines, an industry heat grid, and a thermostat with a jump line.
  - No typography-only scenes.
- **Honesty rules** (research/notes.md):
  - It is told as a fable; we never claim real frogs boil.
  - Kodak and Blockbuster "filed for bankruptcy", never "died".
  - "Blockbuster passed" on the $50M offer; we don't say they "laughed".
