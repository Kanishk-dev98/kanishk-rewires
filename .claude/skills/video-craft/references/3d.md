# Real 3D in Remotion

**Stack (pin exactly):** `@remotion/three` = your Remotion version (tested 4.0.535), `@react-three/fiber` 9.x (tested 9.8.1, the React 19 line), `three` 0.186.x (tested 0.186.1) with `@types/three` 0.186.0. No drei and no `postprocessing` package: everything comes from three's own `examples/jsm`. Kit: `src/three/` (import from `../three`). Render with **`--gl=angle`**.

## 1. What premium 3D needs

1. **3D is a spice.** 1 to 3 real-3D hero moments per film (a logo close-up, a layer stack, a push-through). Everything else stays 2.5D (tilted UI planes with tilt-shift DOF are cheaper and read the same).
2. **Four levers make an object read as premium**: thickness with a lit bevel (~6% of width); a Fresnel rim edge; an environment of a few big soft sources (long specular streaks, not dots); depth of field. Miss one and it looks like plastic.
3. **One lighting rig**: a big soft key from the top-left, a coloured rim from behind, and a never-flat background (corner blooms on light, a corner or horizon glow on dark, on the same diagonal so light ↔ dark cuts feel continuous).
4. **Cameras never stop**: slow drift, dolly or orbit on holds; fast moves only for transitions (push-through 3x in 4 f → 1 f flash → tunnel); every hero arrival settles with ~3% overshoot.
5. **Glass needs something to refract.** On a smooth background glass reads as acrylic. The kit's backdrop has a ~6% hairline grid (square or triangle) that visibly bends through glass.
6. **Use the real logo geometry** (production SVG) in `Logo3D`; never redraw or recolour a real logo.

Minimal scene:
```tsx
const f = useCurrentFrame();
const pose = withDrift(pushSettle({ frame: f, start: 6, dur: 70,
  from: { position: [-1.9, .6, 4.2], target: [-.3, 0, 0], fov: 30 },
  to:   { position: [.75, .22, 3.0], target: [-.32, -.04, 0], fov: 30 } }), f, .025);
<Stage3D mode="light" pose={pose} envRotation={-.4 + f * .004}
  post={{ grain: .02, dof: { focus: focusDistance(pose, [0, 0, 0]), aperture: .0035, maxblur: .008 } }}>
  <Logo3D svg={LOGO_PATH_D} finish="satin" assemble={prog(f, 14, 60)} wave={f} rotation={[.12, -.42, .02]} />
</Stage3D>
```

**Composite with 2D:** 2D under (any AbsoluteFill background) → 3D middle (`<Layer3D pose={pose}>`) → 2D over (captions pinned to 3D points with `projectToScreen(pose, [x, y, z])` → `{x, y}` px; the pose is a pure function, so the pin is frame-exact). On a transparent canvas there is no backdrop to refract and no bloom: use `satin`/`metal` plus `GlowSprite`.

## 3. Determinism (non-negotiable)

- All animation comes from `useCurrentFrame()` passed as props. Never read `clock`, `delta`, `Date` or `Math.random` in `useFrame`. Randomness uses Remotion `random(seed)`.
- The only `useFrame` in the kit is PostFX's render call (priority 1), which only renders.
- Build textures in `useMemo` keyed by content; quantise reveals so text redraws only when a row appears.
- The environment is procedural (PMREM of a softbox scene): no HDR files, no network.

## 4. Material recipes (verified in renders)

| Finish | Numbers | Where it works |
|---|---|---|
| `satin` (lacquer) | metalness 0.05, roughness 0.38, clearcoat 1 / 0.12, sheen 0.4 in the soft accent | light-stage hero; transparent overlays; closest to a flat 2D logo |
| `metal` (anodised accent) | metalness 1, roughness 0.22 (bright face 0.16), clearcoat 1 / 0.08, envMapIntensity 1.4 | accents and small pieces; best "real object" read on light |
| `glass` | transmission 1, ior 1.5, roughness 0.06, thickness 0.22, attenuation accent @ 0.45, clearcoat 1 / 0.08, dispersion 0.15 | dark stage with rim ≥ 1.5 (neon-edged glass) |
| `crystal` (glass on LIGHT) | white base, transmission 1, ior 1.62, roughness 0.015, thickness 0.5, attenuation @ 1.1 to 1.6, dispersion 0.9, envMapIntensity 1.8; with bevel 0.013 × 8 segments, white rim 1.1 (power 3.2), dark edge-absorption shell 0.85 (power 1.25), sheen 0.12 + a frame-driven sweep band | light-stage hero glass; pair with `shadows`, `ShadowFloor`, `GlassCaustic` |
| `frosted` | glass + roughness 0.42, thickness 0.18, attenuation 0.6, clearcoat 0.6 | cards and slabs; UI behind glass |
| Fresnel rim | additive, power 2.4 to 3.2; intensity 0.3 to 0.6 on light, 1.2 to 2.0 on dark | every hero object |
| Slabs on light | frosted, near-white tinted, attenuation soft accent @ 0.55, roughness 0.22 to 0.28 | `LayerStack3D` (pure white frosted slabs vanish on a light stage) |

**Glass on a light stage reads as acrylic until you add:** a sheen gradient (top-left bright) and a moving sweep band (`sweep = -1.3 + f/150*2.6`); dark tinted edges (edge-absorb shell) plus a white Fresnel rim; a clear body (attenuation ≥ 1.1) with high ior and dispersion so the backdrop grid bends with a faint chromatic split; a real soft shadow with an additive caustic pool inside it.

**Do not:** use attenuationDistance < 0.2 on thin glass (black jade); use bloom on a light stage (blows out frosted slabs and text); put a point light in front of glass (glare hot spot).

## 5. Lighting recipes (`env.tsx`)

- **Light stage**: env = mid-grey room (reflections need darks), key panel 5×3 @ 7 top-left, white strips 0.9×7 @ 6 and @ 4.5 left/right, accent strip 6×1 @ 5 behind-right, floor bounce @ 1.4. Direct: hemisphere 0.55, key directional 2.2 at (−2.2, 5, 2.6) (also the shadow caster), accent rim spot 40 at (3.5, 2.5, −3.5), white rim spot 14 at (−3.5, 1.5, −3). Backdrop: tinted light base, accent blooms top-right / bottom-left, lighter spot behind the subject, vignette 0.22, grid @ ~22%.
- **Dark stage**: black room, accent strips @ 4.5 / 6, white top @ 2.4, white kicker @ 1.2; accent rim spot 40 to 60, white rim 30; backdrop near-black with a tinted glow on the same diagonal, vignette 0.55, grid @ 7%.
- **Real shadows** (`shadows` on Stage3D): PCF soft 2048², radius 10, blurSamples 20, bias −0.0004, normalBias 0.02, shadow camera ±3, near 0.5, far 14; `shadowStrength` 0.5 to 0.6 on light; add `<ShadowFloor y={floorY} opacity={0.2 to 0.26}/>`. Cost: one extra depth render per frame.
- **Caustic**: `GlassCaustic` default offset (0.26, −0.31) is where the key throws an object 0.6 above the floor; recompute if you move either.
- **Highlight roll-off** (on by default): values above knee 0.9 compress toward ceiling 2.0 before bloom, so a tiny specular never flashes for one frame; the pass also sanitises Inf/NaN (one bad half-float pixel otherwise spreads into a black frame through bloom). Pair with roughness ≥ 0.06 on dark glass and a rim spot at 40, not 60.
- **Bloom over UI text**: canvas-text materials are `toneMapped={false}` at 1.0, so use bloom threshold ≥ 1.0 whenever readable text is in frame.
- **Tone mapping**: `NeutralToneMapping`, exposure 1 (ACES desaturates and shifts brand hues).
- **The cheapest premium trick**: `envRotation = base + frame * 0.004 to 0.012` sweeps specular streaks across a still object.

## 6. Camera recipes (pure functions of the frame)

| Move | Recipe |
|---|---|
| Hero push with settle | `pushSettle({dur: 60 to 70, overshoot: .03})` (analytic damped curve: one 3% overshoot, exactly 1 at the end) |
| Orbit | `orbit({fromDeg: -28, toDeg: 22, radius: 2.5, height: .25, dur: 150})`, easeInOutCubic |
| Dolly-in | `dolly({from: [.9,.35,3.5], to: [.4,.14,2.55], dur: 150})` |
| Low lateral dolly over a field | `dolly(..., ease: t => t)`, fov 34, DOF on the followed tile |
| Keyed shots in one take | `keyed(frame, [{at, dur, pose, overshoot}])` |
| Never static | wrap any pose in `withDrift(pose, frame, .02 to .03, 240)` |
| Rack focus | `focus = lerp(focusDistance(pose, A), focusDistance(pose, B), easeInOutCubic(prog(f, start, 36)))`, aperture .006, maxblur .012 |
| Push-through | camera z +1.2 → 0 and fov 40 → 63 over 46 f, tunnel streaks `speed: 1 + f/12`, 8 f flash to the light wash |
| Framing | offset `target.x` by −0.3 so the object sits right of centre, clear of a top-left label; fov 30 to 34 (avoid > 45 except in the tunnel) |

**Motion blur** (fast camera moves): `post={{ motionBlur: { prevPose: poseAt(frame - 0.5), strength: 1, maxLength: 0.08 } }}`. Depth reprojection with a 180° shutter, 32 dithered taps, frame-accurate because poses are pure functions; write the pose as a function of a fractional frame so `t − 0.5` is valid. It blurs **camera** motion only; keep fast object moves under 6 f or move the camera instead. (Wrapping a ThreeCanvas in `@remotion/motion-blur` was tested and produced no blur headless.)

**DOF**: PostFX `dof` is three's BokehPass (real depth focus ramp); objects flagged `userData.noDepth` (backdrop, sprites, streaks, rims) are excluded from the depth pass. On a transparent layer, fake it: near and far groups in separate `Layer3D`s with a CSS blur on the far one.

## 7. Headless render

- Always `--gl=angle` for 3D: `npx remotion still src/index.ts <Comp> out.png --frame=N --gl=angle` and `npx remotion render src/index.ts <Comp> out.mp4 --gl=angle --crf=14 --concurrency=4 --timeout=120000`. The default backend timed out on transmission-heavy frames; software `swangle` was flaky.
- `--timeout=120000` covers shader compile + PMREM bake on the first frame of each Sequence.
- Stills take ~16 s each including ~8 s of bundling; batch ≤ 4 stills per command (or bundle once and render stills from the bundle).
- Measured cost (M-series laptop, concurrency 4, CRF 14): ~0.16 to 0.19 s per frame overall for 3D lab scenes; a 60 s all-3D film ≈ 5 min.

## 8. Performance

- One canvas per shot: each `Stage3D` in its own `<Sequence premountFor={0}>` (WebGL contexts cap at ~16; two heavy canvases must not overlap).
- Transmission costs an extra scene pass: keep 1 to 3 transmissive objects in view; use satin/metal for crowds.
- Instance repeated geometry (`TileField3D`: ~1000 tiles = 1 draw call). Build geometry in `useMemo`; never rebuild it per frame.
- Canvas textures at 2048 px wide are enough for ≥ 28 px on screen when the surface spans ≥ 45% of the frame width (author text at ≥ 64 texture px).
- dpr 1; MSAA from the composer target (samples 4). Bloom (UnrealBloomPass) is the most expensive pass: drop it on shots that don't glow.

## 9. 3D QA

1. Render stills (start, middle, end of every 3D shot) and look at each.
2. Material: bevels catch a highlight; a rim separates the object from the background; glass shows refraction (grid lines bend) or a bright edge on dark; metal shows strip reflections. No flat or plastic look.
3. Exposure: no blown whites on light (bloom off); no crushed object on dark (rim ≥ 1.2).
4. Framing: nothing important clipped by the frame or by labels; captions inside the safe area.
5. Text on 3D surfaces ≥ 28 px on screen, otherwise pin a 2D caption with `projectToScreen`.
6. Motion: every hold drifts; hero arrivals settle (3%, no dead stop); no frame-to-frame jitter or single-frame glints (check every-frame tiles over 1 s windows around arrivals and glass rotations); no pop or black frame when a canvas mounts.
7. Shadows: soft penumbra, no acne or banding, under the key's direction. Motion blur: streaks continuous, holds either side sharp. Text3D: no missing glyphs; letters settle onto one baseline.
8. Brand: theme colours only; the logo is the exact production geometry; states are shape + label; backgrounds never flat white.

## 10. Known limits

- Camera motion blur only (per-object temporal blur would need in-canvas sub-frame accumulation).
- `Text3D` has no kerning (advance widths only); glyph geometry rebuilds when `text` or `size` changes, so animate the group's scale, not `size`. Needs a three.js typeface JSON (convert a licensed TTF offline, e.g. with facetype.js; keep the licence file next to it).
- Glass absorption uses one thickness value; edge darkening is a shader approximation.
- Shadows come from the key light only; transmissive objects cast an opaque-tinted shadow (soften with `shadowStrength`).
